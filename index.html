How it works

Whether it's a repair or a refurbished device, the same standard applies every time.

1

Get in touch

Send a quote request with what's wrong (or what you're after), and whether you want pickup, drop-off, or post.

2

Diagnose

Software issues are identified — slow performance, startup problems, malware, OS errors — and you get a clear quote before anything happens.

3

Fix & harden

The issue is resolved, updates applied, and — if you want it — a security baseline set: encryption, firewall, and safer defaults.

4

Hand back

Collected, delivered, or posted back, with a plain-English rundown of what was done.

02

Services

Focused on software — repair, security, and setup for individuals. Not hardware repair, and not aimed at businesses.

Laptop & PC repair Software

Slow performance, startup issues, virus and malware removal, OS reinstalls, driver problems. Hardware faults (screens, keyboards, internals) are outside scope, but you'll be told honestly if that's what's needed.

Device security check-up Individuals

For personal devices only: password manager setup, two-factor authentication, backup review, and a plain-English report on what to fix — ideal before a device holds anything important.
