How it works

Whether it's a repair or a refurbished device, the same standard applies every time.

1

Get in touch

Send a quote request with what's wrong (or what you're after), and whether you'll drop it off locally or send it by post.

2

Diagnose

Software issues are identified — slow performance, startup problems, malware, OS errors — and you get a clear quote before anything happens.

3

Fix & harden

The issue is resolved, updates applied, and — if you want it — a security baseline set: encryption, firewall, and safer defaults.

4

Hand back

Picked up locally or posted back, with a plain-English rundown of what was done.
